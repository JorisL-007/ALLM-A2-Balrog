Interpretation and decision
---------------------------
On Qwen, compact planning has a modest positive paired mean, and critique
greatly reduces observed turn loops at a small marginal token cost, but
neither overall gain survives the twelve-test adjustment. CoT helps goto
under its half-sized seed subset but is very costly and frequently truncated
at 256 tokens; pickup is worse than naive. OLMo lacks enough basic success
to demonstrate useful transfer. The partial Luna data show that the frozen
planner can harm a stronger base model by inducing turn loops; bounded
critique breaks many of those loops but does not exceed naive overall.
Crafter shows no reliable transfer benefit from these BabyAI mechanisms.

The intended full three-model significance panel is incomplete until the
450 missing Luna episodes, including all Crafter episodes, are handled in a
new source-pinned continuation. Keep the one refused CoT case missing; do
not count it as a task loss. The current note is evidence-based for the
completed panels and descriptive for the interrupted Luna prefix.

Behavior and cost
-----------------
Luna planning made 2,266 alternating-turn windows, compared with 270 for
critique and 42 for naive. Critique issued 196 extra reflection calls within
3,737 total calls; planning used 5,033 calls, because it exhausted more
budgets. Of the 77 planning-loss/critique-win pairs, 76 critique episodes
made at least one reflection call; one won without triggering, so paired
outcomes alone should not be read as a pure causal effect of a critic call.
Mean BabyAI episode input/output tokens and elapsed seconds were:
  naive 9,400 / 90 / 18.5 s; CoT 16,415 / 1,032 / 39.4 s;
  planning 22,365 / 1,288 / 38.4 s; critique 15,555 / 989 / 29.6 s.
CoT's mean includes 89 completed episodes; other means use 180. Planning
cost about $0.00288 per BabyAI episode at the recorded rate estimate,
critique $0.00205, and naive $0.00099. Critique's lower total cost than
planning reflects shorter episodes, not a free reflection call. The study's
entire Luna expenditure estimate is $1.53746, including pilots, retries,
and $0.00115 uncertain charges from the two rejected requests. It is not
an API invoice. Luna CoT had one length-limited BabyAI reply and two replies
without a parsed ACTION across 2,244 calls; unlike Qwen CoT, its poor result
is not mainly explained by 256-token truncation.

Decision: the full model-transfer panel strongly rejects a general claim
that frozen compact planning improves BabyAI success. Its turn-loop
failure is pronounced on Luna; bounded reflection repairs many of those
failures but still underperforms the supplied naive policy and costs more.
The smaller Qwen planning gain and OLMo floor therefore do not transfer as
a consistent improvement. Keep the original frozen agents and both refusal
records intact; any next change belongs in a new development study.
