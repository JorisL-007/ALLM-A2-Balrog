# Follow-up research log

## 2026-10-16: hypothesis and pilot setup

The BALROG BabyAI guide says `pick up` acts on an object below the agent and recommends `toggle` for pickup. Installed MiniGrid acts on a portable object in the front cell. Hypothesis: correcting the guide may improve pickup success; a very narrow target-cell substitution may fix residual wrong actions without changing navigation. A one-call goal-state line may reduce loops at lower token cost than the existing extra-call E3 critic. The pilot uses Qwen3-4B, temperature zero, seeds 20–24, `goto`/`pickup`/`open`, 32 actions, and full raw prompts and trajectories.

The first preflight `goto` episode on seed 20 succeeded in 9 actions. Its [raw calls](../experiment_results/followup_pilot_preflight/episodes/babyai/goto/plan/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) show the old conflicting guide. The next invocation stopped before launching an episode because tuple-valued in-memory settings did not compare equal to JSON list-valued settings. I normalized the manifest serialization and preserved the preflight result separately.

Early pilot `goto` seed 20: [frozen plan](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/goto/plan/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) succeeded in 9 actions; [corrected guide](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/goto/guide/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) succeeded in 6. The raw prompt shows the corrected front-cell pickup instruction. The gate stayed inactive on `goto`, as required. The [goal-state prompt](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/goto/guide_goal/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) produced the requested `STATE_VS_GOAL` line and succeeded in 9 actions, but one line falsely said the blue key was not in view while its own `FACTS` line placed the key in view. The line is an LLM assertion, not guaranteed grounded truth.

Early pilot `pickup` seed 20: [frozen plan trajectory](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/plan/seed_020/rep_0/babyai/BabyAI-MixedTrainLocal-v0/pickup/BabyAI-MixedTrainLocal-v0/pickup_run_00.csv) succeeded in 9 actions; after facing the blue key it first proposed `go forward`, then `pick up`. The [corrected-guide trajectory](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide/seed_020/rep_0/babyai/BabyAI-MixedTrainLocal-v0/pickup/BabyAI-MixedTrainLocal-v0/pickup_run_00.csv) failed at the 32-action cap after repeated left/right turns. This is an observed navigation failure that the pickup gate cannot address. A guide correction is not automatically beneficial.

On the same pickup seed, `guide_gate` did not intervene because the agent never faced the exact blue key. Its 32-action outcome remained failure. The [one-call goal-state raw log](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide_goal/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) shows the line did not break the turn loop either: it alternated right and left for at least the first 12 actions and failed at 32. This keeps the gate's causal scope narrow and identifies a limitation of the proposed reflection prompt.

The [history-32 raw log](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide_h32/seed_020/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) reached 1,689 input tokens at its longest call (32 actions), comfortably under the 8,192-token server context even with a 256-token answer allowance. This check is based on recorded server token usage, not a text length estimate.

On `goto` seed 22, [guide-only calls](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/goto/guide/seed_022/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) alternated left and right after the first forward move and failed. [Goal-state calls](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/goto/guide_goal/seed_022/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) took a different route and succeeded in 9 actions. The first goal-state line said a wall six steps forward blocked the current position; that is not literally supported by the observation. The outcome shows a changed action policy, while the text itself is imperfect. On `pickup` seed 22, frozen planning collected a green box in 11 actions after one blocked `go forward`; guide-only again alternated turns and failed at 32. The gate had no eligible target-facing proposal on seeds 20–22.

`pickup` seed 23 revealed temperature-zero variation. The [guide-only raw calls](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide/seed_023/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) and [guide + gate raw calls](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide_gate/seed_023/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) had identical prompts through step 25, but the first proposed `go forward` with a blue ball one step forward and the second proposed `pick up`. The guide-only run failed; the gate-condition run succeeded. **The gate logged zero substitutions**, so that success difference cannot be credited to the rule. The planned threefold temperature-zero repeatability check is necessary for interpretation.

`pickup` seed 24 supplied the positive rule check. At step 4, the [gate raw call](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide_gate/seed_024/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) showed mission `pick up the grey box`, current observation `a grey box 1 step forward`, and model `ACTION: go forward`. The harness submitted `pick up` with reason `mission target grey box observed 1 step forward`; BALROG executed `pick up`, the next observation said `You carry a grey box`, and the episode succeeded in 5 actions. [Guide-only](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide/seed_024/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) also succeeded, but needed 17 actions after repeatedly trying to enter the box cell. Across the five pilot seeds, gate runs on `goto` and `open` recorded no substitutions. This is a concrete behavior change and a measured step reduction on one seed, not a general success-rate claim.

## Pilot summary and freeze

The [pilot matrix](../experiment_results/followup_pilot/performance_matrix.csv) covers all 75 planned episodes. Successes out of five were:

| Task | Frozen plan | Corrected guide | Guide + pickup rule | Guide + goal-state line | Guide, history 32 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `goto` | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 3 |
| `pickup` | 4 | 1 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| `open` | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 0 |

These are development observations, not estimates of final effects. Guide + gate made exactly one substitution in 5 pickup episodes and none in 10 `goto`/`open` episodes. The history-32 maximum input call was 1,689 tokens. Total pilot episode time was 816 seconds (10.9 seconds per episode); a simple extrapolation over 1,760 fixed evaluation episodes gives 5.3 hours, with longer 128-action transfer tasks adding uncertainty. All pilot token totals matched their raw call logs and all output directories passed runner verification. A portable nine-call sample, including successful and failed trajectories, is in [followup_raw_llm_samples.jsonl](followup_raw_llm_samples.jsonl).

After the pilot, the only runner setting changed was to give the naive control the same 256-token output cap as the custom conditions. The runner now refreshes partial summaries after each invocation. The final panel manifest pins the resulting code and prompt hashes. No agent behavior will be revised in response to held-out results.

During the first held-out seed-30 preflight, I caught a reporting error: the summary grouped task and condition without panel, which would merge 32-action primary episodes with 128-action transfer episodes. The 21 completed preflight episodes and one interrupted attempt are preserved under `experiment_results/followup_preflight_summary_fix/`. I added a panel field to each completion record and grouped matrices by panel, verified the analyzer on all 75 pilot records, then started a new clean final directory. Agent prompts and action logic were unchanged. The analyzer's own SHA is recorded beside every summary; the runner manifest pins behavior-affecting code.

## 2026-10-17: frozen evaluation and interpretation

The fixed schedule finished all 1,760 episodes in 5.77 hours. The corrected guide's primary `pickup` result was 29/60 against 34/60 for frozen planning (paired −8.3 points; exact McNemar p=0.424). The hypothesis that correcting the instruction alone would improve mission success was **not supported**. Inspected [seed 32](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide/seed_032/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) favored guide, while [seed 34](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide/seed_034/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) lost because guide kept turning.

The pickup gate made 15 primary substitutions; every one led to an observed carry and pickup success, but guide + gate had the same 29/60 overall successes as guide alone. This supports the narrow action-mechanism hypothesis while leaving the broader performance hypothesis unsupported. On [seed 33](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/pickup/guide_gate/seed_033/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl), the yellow key was directly ahead, the model proposed `go forward`, and the harness executed `pick up`. In the broader panel, one gate substitution occurred on a compound mission that began `pick up`; none occurred on missions that began `go to`, `open`, or `put`.

The one-call goal-state prompt added cost without a robust success gain: 3.71M versus 3.45M primary tokens and 37.3 versus 27.3 minutes, with a three-task paired difference of −0.6 points. [Goto seed 31](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/goto/guide_goal/seed_031/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) repeated a turn loop even while its goal-state line stated there was no progress. Its Crafter mean achievement progression was 0.0500 versus 0.0591 for frozen planning, at higher token and time cost.

History 4 used 2.14M versus 3.45M tokens for corrected-guide history 16 and had 13/60 versus 3/60 `open` successes. The prespecified three-task difference (+7.2 points) had Holm-adjusted p=0.320, so the open gain remains exploratory. On [open seed 35](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/open/guide_h4/seed_035/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl), history 4 carried the purple key and opened its door in nine actions; history 16 carried the key but moved away and failed. A new fixed open-task panel is the clearest next experiment.

The E3 critic reduced descriptive alternating-turn windows from 1,210 to 156 relative to frozen planning with similar total token use, but its success gain across the primary tasks was only 80 versus 75 of 180. The critic's 197 reflection-mode calls supplied the action directly on their trigger steps, so they were counted but did not add calls beyond the action count. [Goto seed 30](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/goto/e3/seed_030/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl) shows a late reflection at step 27 that did not recover before the 32-action cap.

The [repeatability matrix](../experiment_results/followup_v1/repeatability_matrix.csv) found success variation in 3/20 task-seed-condition cells when the primary run and three later repeats were compared. On [pickup seed 31](../experiment_results/followup_v1/episodes/babyai/pickup/plan/seed_031/rep_0/llm_calls.jsonl), the first prompt matched [repeat one](../experiment_results/followup_v1/repeat/babyai/pickup/plan/seed_031/rep_1/llm_calls.jsonl), but the model's first action changed and the outcome flipped from success to failure. This limits attribution of single paired outcome differences to a harness change. See the [final report](followup_report.md) for full matrices, intervals, costs, and the inspected transfer trajectories.
