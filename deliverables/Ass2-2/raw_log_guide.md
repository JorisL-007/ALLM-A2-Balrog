# Raw LLM examples for manual inspection

`raw_llm_samples.jsonl` contains seven complete custom-agent API calls, including every message sent to the model, the unparsed reply, token counts, call role, and source file. Lines 1–3 show the development turn cycle before critique and the two critiques that broke it; line 4 shows a held-out planning call during an `open` task; line 5 shows a held-out reflection call; line 6 is the Qwen3-14B critique that broke seed 12's `goto` turn cycle; line 7 is Phi-4's seed 12 `pickup` critique that turned away from the adjacent key. The `source` field points to the full JSONL for that run. Compare these with the same run's BALROG trajectory CSV for the actual executed action and next observation. Every final custom-agent episode has its own full `seed_*/llm_calls.jsonl` in `experiment_results/`.
